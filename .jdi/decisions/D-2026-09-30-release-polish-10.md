D-2026-09-30-release-polish-10 (2026-09-30): After a cancelled rev C upload Bezel sends no filler. On the 8.8" (ROM 1.90, 29.7 GiB card) completing a cancelled 41.6 MB upload with 25 MB of filler right after the cancel made the firmware stop reading after ~30.8 MB in all and stay hung until a USB replug, while a cancel without filler leaves a screen that reconnects on the next command (wake) and stray bytes that only reach the next upload, where the size check catches them. Recovery is HELLO + GET_FILE_SIZE as before T-7.3; the user deletes the partial, and a next upload that fails its size check says to delete it and send again. Whether large fast uploads to the card can hang the firmware on their own (pacing) is checked with the user present in T-7.8 before 1.0.
