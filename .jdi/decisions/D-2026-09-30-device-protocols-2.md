D-2026-09-30-device-protocols-2 (2026-09-30): Automatic hardware traffic is limited to what the vendor apps send on every start and stop: HELLO, STOP_VIDEO/STOP_MEDIA, brightness, PRE/END update (0x86/0x87), frames, QUERY_STATUS and screen off. RESTART (0x84), 0x82, persistent OPTIONS (0x7D) and ROTATION (0x81), storage writes, firmware and the CH552 0xC9 command are never sent implicitly: they need an explicit command and, when destructive, Confirm::Yes.
