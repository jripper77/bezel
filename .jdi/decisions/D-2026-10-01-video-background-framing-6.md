D-2026-10-01-video-background-framing-6 (2026-10-01): Editing the framing in the studio. The inspector's video background gains a Framing group: Rotation (Auto showing the detected angle, 0, 90, 180, 270 degrees), Fill or Fit (cover or contain), Zoom (slider and number, 100 to 400 %, step 5 %), Position X and Y (0 to 100 %) with Center, Pad color (shown with Fit only) and Reset framing. A "Frame on canvas" toggle (aria-pressed) puts the editing area in framing mode: dragging pans the position, the wheel zooms around the pointer, arrows move 1 % (Shift: 10 %), + and - zoom 5 %, 0 resets, Esc or Enter leave; elements cannot be selected meanwhile and an aria-live line says the zoom and position. Each control change and each drag or wheel burst is one undo step through the store's gestures (undo and redo as everywhere else). Every string exists in pt-BR and en with key parity. The demo backend gains a landscape theme with a pre-turned 480x1920 video and a simulated decoder, so Playwright (4 projects, axe) covers Auto, the controls, the canvas, undo/redo and playback. The CLI only honours the framing (`bezel run`, sending a theme's video); editing it outside the studio is out of scope. Decided in auto mode (/jdi-issue).
