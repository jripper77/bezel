D-2026-09-30-release-polish-13 (2026-09-30): Bezel restarts a hung rev C screen without a USB replug through its MCU: the six bytes 00 00 00 00 00 c9 written to the MCU port (as the vendor's reconnect ladder does), the port held 8 s, then the SoC is awaited back on the bus. On the 8.8" the SoC left the bus at once and returned about 10 s later, healthy, both from a working and from a hung state (the firmware had stopped reading). It runs on request (`bezel restart`, the studio's Restart screen, offered by errors that leave the screen hung) and automatically, once per failure, when the SoC stopped reading (write stall) or an enumerated SoC answers no HELLO after the wake. For this command it supersedes D-2026-09-30-device-protocols-2 ("MCU only on explicit action"). Disruptive (what plays stops), never destructive (stored files stay). The USB device reset (vendor ladder) is not used: it needs root on Linux.
