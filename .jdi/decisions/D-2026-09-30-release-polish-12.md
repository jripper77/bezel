D-2026-09-30-release-polish-12 (2026-09-30): Rev C uploads are capped at 25 MiB (26,214,400 bytes) per file. On the 8.8" (ROM 1.90) the firmware keeps an upload in memory and stops reading at exactly 29,577,216 bytes whatever the rate (7 MiB/s and 0.94 MiB/s left the same partial file, three times), then stays hung until restarted (D-13); 24.0 MiB passed and the vendor app stored 24.6 MiB files. Pacing does not help and is not done. The cap is part of the upload profile (TUR_USB keeps the vendor's 120 MB): preflight refuses a larger file before anything is sent, a conversion caps the bitrate from the source's duration so its output fits, and a converted file still over the cap is refused before sending, with how to make it fit (shorter clip, lower fps).
